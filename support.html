Support

Braceloft is a calm demolition puzzle: cut the joints, brace with ropes and bring the old frames down without touching the glass garden.

Stuck on a level?

Tap the ? button. After 2 tries the result screen offers a hint; you can unlock the first step with a short video or with brass.

Purchases

Remove Ads and the cosmetic looks are one-time purchases. On a new phone open Shop → Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store.

Your data

Progress is saved on your device. To delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the Game. See the Privacy Policy.
